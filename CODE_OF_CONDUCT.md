# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific when discussing this project. Welcome contributors regardless of background or experience. Critique ideas and code without personal attacks.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized content, spam, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. Remove secrets and personal data from reports and screenshots.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep repository spaces productive and safe. Moderation should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

For repository moderation, contact [@NeonTideX](https://github.com/NeonTideX) in the relevant issue or pull request without sharing private information. Use GitHub's report-abuse tools for harassment or private-information disclosures that need platform intervention.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces hosted by this repository.
